# Thai Silk

## Rationale
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The dyeing of silk yarn with lac dye based on local wisdom still faces challenges regarding uneven dye coloration and potential inaccuracies in pattern design. This study aimed to investigate the effects of the pH of lac dye solutions on silk yarn dyeing and laccaic acid concentration, as well as to develop an application for color database management and silk pattern design using the Flutter Framework. The coloring substances were extracted from lac, and the pH was adjusted within the range of 2.5–7.0. Absorbance was measured using a UV–Visible Spectrophotometer at a wavelength of 488 nm. The concentration of laccaic acid was calculated using a standard calibration equation, and user satisfaction with the application was evaluated among 30 participants.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The pH significantly affected absorbance, laccaic acid concentration, and the color of the dyed silk yarn (p < 0.05). The lac dye solution at pH 7.0 exhibited the highest absorbance of 2.79 and the highest average laccaic acid concentration of 182 ± 3 mg/L, whereas pH 2.5 showed the lowest absorbance of 1.20 and the lowest average laccaic acid concentration of 78 ± 1 mg/L. In addition, the brightness of the dyed silk yarn decreased from pH 2.5 to pH 5.5 and increased from pH 6.0 to pH 7.0.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The application provides features for color database management, dyeing formula recommendations, digital grid-based silk pattern design, and PDF export. Users reported the highest level of satisfaction, with a mean satisfaction score of 4.61 ± 0.56. The findings provide new knowledge regarding the relationship between pH, laccaic acid concentration, and the brightness of lac-dyed silk yarn. Furthermore, the developed application can help reduce inconsistencies in the dyeing process, support silk pattern design, and contribute to the preservation of Thai silk-weaving knowledge through digital technology.

## Objectives
1.	To investigate the effects of changes in the pH of lac dye solutions on the dyeing of silk yarn.
2.	To investigate the laccaic acid concentration of lac dye solutions at different pH levels.
3.	To develop an application for creating a dye color database from lac dye solutions and designing silk patterns using the Flutter Framework.
